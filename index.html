Five directions for ronb.co

Each concept is a complete little site: a home page, a blog archive and a sample article, with light and dark modes. Open any one of them and a switcher bar at the bottom of the screen flips between all five — keeping whichever page you are on, so you can compare the same article straight across. No gradients, no glow, nothing centred: every layout is built on a left-aligned grid.

  • 03

    Index

    Tightened version of the one you liked: sticky identity rail, one narrow column, hairlines and mono metadata. Same skeleton, ~40% less vertical space, denser archive rows and a shorter reading measure.

    Home Writing Article

  • 04

    Ledger

    Same family, run as a record book. The rail is gone in favour of a flat top bar; the archive is a ruled four-column table (date, title, topic, length) and the article sits beside a sticky meta column with a numbered contents list.

    Home Writing Article

  • 05

    Split

    A full-height panel pinned to the left edge holds identity, navigation and the context for whatever you are reading; the content stream scrolls beside it. One hairline divider, flat panel colour, no page header at all.

    Home Writing Article

  • 06

    Broadsheet

    A twelve-column editorial grid, left-aligned and asymmetric: a lead story across seven columns, a stack of shorts beside it, then dense ruled rows. Sharp serif display against a sans body, black and white and one red.

    Home Writing Article

  • 07

    Workbench

    Three panes, edge to edge: a navigation rail with counts, the article, and a contents pane that tracks the section you are reading as you scroll. The densest of the five, and the best of them for a long post.

    Home Writing Article

Sample copy is placeholder. Each design folder is self-contained — copy one out and wire it to your content. The switcher bar is preview-only: delete switcher.js and the one script tag per page when you pick a direction.
