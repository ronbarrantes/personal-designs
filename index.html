Three directions for ronb.co

Each concept is a complete little site: a home page, a blog archive and a sample article, with light and dark modes. Open any one of them and a switcher bar at the bottom of the screen flips between the three — keeping whichever page you are on, so you can compare the same article across all three directions.

  • 01

    Quiet Editorial

    Warm paper, serif display type and a long measure. Articles get a drop cap and a hanging margin for sidenotes — closest to a literary magazine, built for people who actually read to the end.

    Home Writing Article

  • 02

    Soft Horizon

    Centred, airy and rounded, with one soft gradient behind the top of the page and gentle card depth below it. Sans-serif throughout, a sticky translucent nav and a reading-progress line on articles.

    Home Writing Article

  • 03

    Index & Archive

    A sticky identity rail on the left, one narrow column of content on the right. Hairlines, mono metadata and a numbered archive; article text is set in a serif for reading comfort. Precise rather than loud.

    Home Writing Article

Sample copy is placeholder. Each design folder is self-contained — copy one out and wire it to your content. The switcher bar is preview-only: delete switcher.js and the one script tag per page when you pick a direction.
