About

I’m a software engineer who cares a little too much about the seams — error messages, empty states, the shape of a function signature. I’ve spent the last decade building web applications, and most of what I know came from maintaining something long after the launch party ended.

This site is mostly a place to think out loud. If something here is useful, wrong, or both, I’d like to hear about it.
