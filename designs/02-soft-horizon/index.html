About

I’m a software engineer in San Antonio. I’ve spent the last decade building web applications, and most of what I know came from maintaining something long after the launch party ended.

These days I care most about the seams — error messages, empty states, and the shape of a function signature. This site is where I think out loud about them.

A letter, roughly monthly

New essays, plus the two or three things I read that changed my mind. No tracking, no cadence guilt, one-click unsubscribe.
